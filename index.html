06 / Data provenance and assumptions

Embedded values from the uploaded v1.0_Asia_Standard_Cost_Model_GCLA_Pilot (1).xlsx: “v1.0 PEMDO Simul” rows 5–18; “P - v1.0 Simul” rows 5–27; “E - v1.0 Simul” rows 5–22; “M - v1.0 Simul” rows 5–15. Personnel base rate and overtime are calibrated to the workbook’s proposed personnel standard; no extra headcount reduction or OT efficiency benefit is assumed beyond the selected productivity input.

Volume scenario assumes no capacity step-up costs, additional shift premiums, investment, or changes in sales mix. Maintenance and Others allocation is a switchable management assumption. Currency FX held at 26,604 VND/USD. All figures are preliminary and subject to CFO/Operations validation.
